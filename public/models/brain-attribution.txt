Interactive adaptation: The cortex, cerebellum and brainstem were sampled into a lightweight 3D point cloud, reoriented and scaled. The derived brain-network.json geometry is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Gold business-area groupings are illustrative and do not represent anatomical functions.
